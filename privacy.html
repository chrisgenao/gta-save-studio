Privacy · Optional website analytics

Privacy & Analytics

GTA Save Studio edits save files locally in your web browser. We do not upload or store your GTA save files on a server.

Your save files

When you choose a save, the browser reads it into temporary memory so the editor can show fields and export a modified copy. Your file contents, filename, game statistics, character values, and changes are not transmitted to our analytics service. The editor does not create an online save library.

Optional Google Analytics

When configured by the site owner, Google Analytics 4 may be offered to visitors as an optional choice. Its scripts do not load unless you explicitly choose Allow statistics. If you opt in, the website sends page views and limited editor events: a successful import, export, or failed import, with only a game identifier. It does not send filenames, save content, player values, or error text. Google may process IP address and other technical information as described in Google's privacy policy. Google Analytics may store cookies on your device.

Choosing No thanks disables Google Analytics collection for the site. Your choice is stored in browser localStorage, solely to remember your preference. It does not store your save files. You can change your choice below; clearing your browser's cookies or storage also resets the preference. Changing from accept to decline stops further tracking. To delete existing analytics cookies, clear cookies for this site in your browser settings.

Search engines and hosting

The site is served as static files on GitHub Pages. Search engines may crawl its public pages. Hosting providers may keep access and security logs under their own policies. We do not operate an account system or server-side save-file storage.
